About MD Anamul Hoque

I was born and raised in Kroirgacha village of Harirampur union, Gobindganj upazila, Gaibandha district. I began my education in the village primary school. Later, I completed SSC from Gaibandha Govt. Technical School and College and a Diploma in Mechanical Technology from Bogura Polytechnic Institute. I’m currently living in Gazipur, preparing for a job career. My biggest inspirations are my parents. Their love, sacrifice, and way of life continue to guide me forward. I try to improve myself every day, acknowledging my limitations and working to overcome them. I come from a middle-class family, so I dream with caution. I don’t seek luxury before necessity. Every step is tied to responsibility. Even when poverty breaks me, I never lose hope. My self-respect is my strength.
