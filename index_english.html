About Anamul Hoque

I was born and raised in the village of Kroirgacha, Harirampur Union, Gobindganj, Gaibandha. I completed my primary education in my village's school. Later, I graduated from Gaibandha Technical School & College with an SSC in 2020 and completed my Diploma in Mechanical Technology from Bogura Polytechnic Institute. Currently, I am preparing for a job while studying in Gazipur. It is the inspiration from my father and mother that gives me the courage to move forward. Their love, sacrifices, and life philosophies are the guiding principles of my life. I aim to improve and grow myself daily, accepting my limitations. As a middle-class person, I dream with caution. I don't desire luxury before need, and every step I take is bound by responsibility. Though adversity brings tears, I never lose hope. I carry my self-respect above all.
